The floating glass tab bar on Home, Learn and Journal, with a separate round button for importing a puzzle from a photo.

## What you provide

Which of the three tabs is selected: Play, Learn or Journal.

## Rules

- A glass capsule `tabbar-h` tall with `r-bar` corners, `space-5` from the left, right and bottom edges. Content scrolls underneath it.
- Three equal tabs, each an icon over a `caption` label.
- The selected tab sits on a `pill`, in `accent`, with a 700 label and a 2.2 stroke icon. At night the pill is clear, so the colour and weight carry it.
- The round button is its own glass circle of the same height, 10pt to the right of the capsule.
- The game screen has no tab bar. It uses the same glass for a 44pt round Back button, and a 44pt capsule holding Night and More.

## Don't

- Don't add a fourth tab; Night lives behind the moon button on every screen.
- Don't place the bar on top of another glass control.
