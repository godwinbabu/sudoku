# App icon

The icon is a 3×3 box of solid blocks with the answer 5 in the centre. The files are in `icon/` and in the `app-icon` asset group. Do not redraw them.

## Rules

- The same artwork serves the light and dark appearances. `AppIcon-tinted` is the greyscale version that iOS tints.
- The files are square and unmasked. iOS applies the corner shape. In mock-ups, round the corners at 22.5% of the width.
- The colors are the fixed `icon-…` tokens. They do not change with the theme.
- At 29 pt, the centre block must still read as the brightest thing.
- Do not recolor the icon or add the app's name to it.
- Keep a gap around the icon on a background close to `icon-bg`.
