The app icon, "The proof": a 3×3 box of solid blocks with the answer 5 in the centre.

## What you provide

Nothing. Use the files in the `app-icon` asset group as they are.

## Rules

- The same artwork serves the light and dark appearances. Use `AppIcon-tinted` for the iOS tinted appearance.
- The files are square and unmasked; let iOS apply the corner shape. In mock-ups, round the corners at 22.5% of the width.
- The colours are the fixed `icon-…` tokens and do not change with the theme.
- Check it at 29pt: the centre block must still read as the brightest thing.

## Don't

- Don't redraw it, recolour it or add the app's name to it.
- Don't put it on a background close to `icon-bg` without a gap around it.
